    How to play

    You coach the team in the near half. Your players move on their own; you make the three calls that decide each rally.

    Rather just see it first?

    1. Serve. Move the pointer over the far court to aim, then click or tap (or press Space). The power bar swings up and down: a harder serve is tougher to pass but less accurate.
    2. Set. When your team passes, numbers appear over your front-row hitters. Click one, or press 1 to 3, to choose who gets the ball. If you don't, the setter decides.
    3. Attack. While the set is in the air, quickly pick a shot with the buttons or keys 1 to 6, then click where it should land: Power is fastest; Line and Cross aim themselves down the sideline or across court; Roll is a slow, high shot over the block into open space; Tip drops it just over the net; Tool hits off the blockers' hands to send the ball out. If you don't pick a spot, your hitter misses and the point goes to the other team. Aiming right on the lines is risky.
    4. Online. Choose Play online, create a room and send the code or invite link to a friend. Each of you makes the calls for your own team, and you both see the court from your own end. If the connection drops, the game waits up to 30 seconds for it to come back.
    5. Block. When the other team passes, call your block with the buttons or keys 1 to 4. Commit Left, Middle or Right to stack all three blockers there before the set (a great block if you guess right, an open net if you don't), or choose Read to react with two blockers.

    Rally scoring, win each set by two. Before a match, choose the points per set (5, 11 or 15) and how many sets to play (1, best of 3 or best of 5). Winning the rally on the other team's serve wins you the serve and rotates your team. Press P or Esc to pause.

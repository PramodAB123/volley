About these guides

The guides are written by the team that makes Spike Rally, a free 6v6 volleyball game for phone and PC. The rules described are those of indoor volleyball under international (FIVB) rules unless a guide says otherwise; school, college and club leagues sometimes use small variations, so check your own league’s rules for details such as substitutions and libero serving.

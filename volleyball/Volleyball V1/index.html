    How to play

    You coach the team in the near half. Your players move on their own; you make the four calls that decide each rally.

    Rather just see it first?

      Serve

      Aim on their court, then serve while the power bar swings. A harder serve is tougher to pass but less accurate.

      PCMouse to aim, click or SpacePhoneTap a spot on their court

    The six shots

    1PowerFastest, hardest spike. You pick the spot.
    2LineAims itself straight down the sideline.
    3CrossAims itself on a diagonal across court.
    4RollSlow and high over the block into space.
    5TipDrops just over the net. Shift + click or long-press tips anywhere.
    6ToolOff the blockers' hands and out.

    Rules and more

    • Rally scoring: every rally wins a point. Win each set by two.
    • Choose 5, 11 or 15 points per set and 1, 3 or 5 sets before a match.
    • Win a rally on their serve to take the serve; your team rotates.
    • Online: create a room and send the code or invite link. If the connection drops, the game waits up to 30 seconds.
    • Press P or Esc to pause. Stars: 1 per win, +3 for a league title, +5 for a cup. Spend them in the Shop.

    How a rally works

    Your players move on their own. You make the four calls that win or lose each point.

    1

    Serve

    Aim anywhere on their court and serve while the power bar swings. A harder serve is tougher to pass but easier to miss, and the seams between two passers are the weak spots.

    PC mouse to aim, click or Space Phone tap a spot

    2

    Set and attack

    When your team passes, pick which front-row hitter gets the set. While the ball is in the air, choose one of six shots and where it lands. Aim away from the block, but not too close to the lines.

    PC keys 1–6, then click Phone tap a shot, then a spot

    3

    Block and dig

    When the other team attacks, stack your three blockers left, middle or right, or choose Read and react with two. Guess right for a stuff block; guess wrong and your back row has to dig it.

    PC keys 1–4 Phone tap a block button

    New to volleyball? The guided demo walks you through a real rally, one call at a time.

    Six attack shots

    Every spike is a choice. Read the block and the defence, then pick the shot that beats them.

    PowerThe fastest, hardest spike. Best when the block is late.
    LineStraight down the sideline when the block leaves it open.
    CrossA sharp diagonal when the block sits on the line.
    RollSlow and high over the block into open space.
    TipA soft drop just over the net while defenders are deep.
    ToolOff the blockers' hands and out when a big block is waiting.

    Questions

    Is Spike Rally free?

    Yes. The whole game is free to play in your browser, with nothing to download or buy.

    Can I play volleyball online with a friend?

    Yes. Choose Play online, create a room and send your friend the six-character code or the invite link. You each coach one team. If the connection drops, the game waits up to 30 seconds for you to come back.

    Does it work on my phone?

    Yes. Spike Rally runs in any modern browser on Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows, Mac and Chromebook. You can also add it to your home screen to open it like an app.

    What are the controls?

    On a computer use the mouse plus Space and the number keys. On a phone just tap. P or Esc pauses. The how to play guide has every control.

    How do I get better?

    Serve at the seams, set away from the block, and mix your shots so the other coach can't read you. Try the guided demo to practise each call.

    How to play

    You coach the team in the near half. Your players move on their own; you make the four calls that decide each rally.

    Rather just see it first?

    1. 1

      Serve

      Aim on their court, then serve while the power bar swings. A harder serve is tougher to pass but less accurate.

      PCMouse to aim, click or SpacePhoneTap a spot on their court

    2. 2

      Set

      When your team passes, numbers appear over your front-row hitters. Pick who gets the ball, or the setter decides.

      PCClick a player or 1–3PhoneTap a numbered player

    3. 3

      Attack

      While the set is in the air, pick one of six shots, then pick where it lands. Aiming right on the lines is risky.

      PC1–6, then click a spotPhoneTap a shot, then a spot

    4. 4

      Block

      When they pass, commit Left, Middle or Right to stack all three blockers there, or choose Read to react with two.

      PC1–4 or click a B zonePhoneTap a block button

    The six shots

    1PowerFastest, hardest spike. You pick the spot.
    2LineAims itself straight down the sideline.
    3CrossAims itself on a diagonal across court.
    4RollSlow and high over the block into space.
    5TipDrops just over the net. Shift + click or long-press tips anywhere.
    6ToolOff the blockers' hands and out.

    Rules and more

    • Rally scoring: every rally wins a point. Win each set by two.
    • Choose 5, 11 or 15 points per set and 1, 3 or 5 sets before a match.
    • Win a rally on their serve to take the serve; your team rotates.
    • Online: create a room and send the code or invite link. If the connection drops, the game waits up to 30 seconds.
    • Press P or Esc to pause. Stars: 1 per win, +3 for a league title, +5 for a cup. Spend them in the Shop.

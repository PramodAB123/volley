Contact

Bugs, ideas, feedback or a business question? We read every message.

Email us YOUR-EMAIL@example.com We usually reply within a few working days.

Reporting a bug

It helps a lot if you include:

  • what you were doing when it happened (for example, the mode and the score);
  • your device and browser (for example, "Android phone, Chrome" or "Windows laptop, Edge");
  • a screenshot, if you can.

Ideas and feedback

New modes, teams, courts, controls: tell us what would make Spike Rally more fun. Check how to play first in case it is already in the game.

Privacy requests

To ask what data relates to you, or to have it deleted, email us with the subject "Privacy request". Most game data lives only in your own browser, and you can delete it yourself any time with Stats > Reset progress or by clearing this site's data in your browser. More in the privacy policy.

Press and business

For partnerships, licensing, portals or press, use the same address with a short note about what you have in mind.

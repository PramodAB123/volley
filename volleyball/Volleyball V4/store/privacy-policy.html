Privacy policy

Spike Rally for Android (package buzz.spikerally.game) · Last updated 12 November 2026

Spike Rally is a single-player volleyball game. This policy explains what the Android app does with information. In short: the game itself collects nothing. The only exception is the optional reward videos, which are shown by Google AdMob when you choose to watch one.

Information the app collects

The game itself collects nothing. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no online play, and it contains no analytics, tracking or crash reporting software of its own.

Ads

After a match you can choose to watch a short video ad to double your reward, or to replay a lost tournament match. Ads are only shown when you tap one of these buttons. They are provided by Google AdMob, which uses an internet connection and may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), device information and how you interact with the ad, to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. This data is collected and handled by Google, not by us. Google's privacy policy explains how: policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the app saves game data in its own private storage on your device: stars, unlocked balls, courts and jerseys, career, league and tournament progress, match stats, your daily reward streak and your settings. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

If your phone's own Android backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your personal Google backup so that your progress returns on a new phone. That backup is controlled by your Google account settings; we cannot access it.

Deleting your data

You can delete all game data at any time: in the app use Stats > Reset progress, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app does not ask for any permissions that need your approval. It uses the internet only to load the ads you choose to watch. It does not access your GPS location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases. Diamonds and coins are earned only by playing (and by the optional reward videos).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone, and we do not knowingly allow ads to be personalised for children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if a future version adds online features), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

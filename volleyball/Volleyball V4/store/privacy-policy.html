Privacy policy

Spike Rally for Android (package buzz.spikerally.game) · Last updated 6 November 2026

Spike Rally is a single-player volleyball game. This policy explains what the Android app does with information. In short: it collects nothing and shares nothing.

Information the app collects

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no online play, and makes no network requests. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash reporting software, and it does not use the Android advertising ID.

Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the app saves game data in its own private storage on your device: stars, unlocked balls, courts and jerseys, career, league and tournament progress, match stats, your daily reward streak and your settings. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

If your phone's own Android backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your personal Google backup so that your progress returns on a new phone. That backup is controlled by your Google account settings; we cannot access it.

Deleting your data

You can delete all game data at any time: in the app use Stats > Reset progress, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app does not ask for any permissions that need your approval. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases. Stars are earned only by playing.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if a future version adds online features), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

          Privacy policy

          Spike Rally for Android · Last updated 12 November 2026

          Spike Rally is a single-player volleyball game. It works offline. The game itself does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. Optional reward videos are served by Google AdMob (see below).

          Ads

          You can choose to watch a short video ad to double a match reward or to replay a lost tournament match. Ads are only shown when you tap one of these buttons. They are provided by Google AdMob, which needs an internet connection and may use your device's advertising ID and basic device information to show and measure ads. Google's privacy policy explains how it handles that data: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

          What the game stores

          Your progress (your player name and avatar, level, diamonds, coins, unlocked balls, courts and jerseys, career, league and tournament progress, stats, daily reward streak and settings) is saved only on this device, inside the app's own storage. It never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. You can also wipe it any time with Stats > Reset progress.

          What the game does not do

          • No accounts, sign-in or online play.
          • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting SDKs of its own, and no ads unless you choose to watch one.
          • No in-app purchases. Diamonds and coins are earned by playing and cannot be bought.
          • The internet is only used to load the ads you choose to watch. The app asks for no permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

          Children

          The game is not directed at children under 13. The game itself collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

          Changes and contact

          If this policy changes, the new version will ship with an app update and be posted at the policy web address on the Google Play listing. Questions: see the developer contact on the Google Play listing.

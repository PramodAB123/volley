          About

          Spike RallyVersion 1.0.1

          A 6v6 volleyball coaching game. Your players move on their own; you make the calls that decide every rally: the serve, the set, the spike and the block.

          • OfflinePlays anywhere, no internet needed
          • No adsNothing gets in the way of a rally
          • No accountYour progress stays on this phone

          Win matches to earn coins, XP and diamonds. Level up, unlock courts and balls, build your own team and fill your trophy cabinet.

          Privacy policy

          Spike Rally for Android · Last updated 6 November 2026

          Spike Rally is a single-player volleyball game. It works fully offline and does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

          What the game stores

          Your progress (your player name and avatar, level, diamonds, coins, unlocked balls, courts and jerseys, career, league and tournament progress, stats, daily reward streak and settings) is saved only on this device, inside the app's own storage. It never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. You can also wipe it any time with Stats > Reset progress.

          What the game does not do

          • No accounts, sign-in or online play.
          • No ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting SDKs.
          • No in-app purchases. Diamonds and coins are earned by playing and cannot be bought.
          • No internet access is used. The app asks for no permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

          Children

          The game is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age.

          Changes and contact

          If this policy changes, the new version will ship with an app update and be posted at the policy web address on the Google Play listing. Questions: see the developer contact on the Google Play listing.

          How to play

          You coach the team in the near half. Your players move on their own; you make the four calls that decide each rally.

          Rather just see it first?

          1. 1

            Serve

            Aim on their court, then serve while the power bar swings. A harder serve is tougher to pass but less accurate.

            PCMouse to aim, click or SpacePhoneTap a spot on their court

          2. 2

            Set

            When your team passes, numbers appear over your front-row hitters. Pick who gets the ball, or the setter decides.

            PCClick a player or 1–3PhoneTap a numbered player

          3. 3

            Attack

            While the set is in the air, pick where the spike lands. Every spike is a Power hit straight at that spot. Pick no spot and your hitter misses. Aiming right on the lines is risky.

            PCClick a spot on their courtPhoneTap a spot on their court

          4. 4

            Block

            When they pass, commit Left, Middle or Right to stack all three blockers there, or choose Read to react with two.

            PC1–4 or click a B zonePhoneTap a block button

          Rules and more

          • Rally scoring: every rally wins a point. Win each set by two.
          • Choose 5, 11 or 15 points per set and 1, 3 or 5 sets before a match.
          • Win a rally on their serve to take the serve; your team rotates.
          • Press P or Esc to pause (on a phone, use the pause button or the Back button). Every match earns coins and XP; wins, titles, daily gifts and level-ups give diamonds. Spend them in the Shop.
